BITEDASH - REFUND, CANCELLATION & QUALITY ISSUES POLICY

1. Order Cancellations
- Free Cancellation: Customers can cancel an order within 60 seconds of placing it with a 100% full refund.
- Post-Preparation Cancellation: If an order is canceled after 60 seconds or once the restaurant has accepted it, a 100% cancellation fee applies, and no refund will be issued.

2. Damaged, Spilled, or Missing Items
- Photo Proof Requirement: Customers must report missing, damaged, or spilled food within 30 minutes of delivery via the chat interface along with a photograph.
- Spilled Food: If food packaging is severely damaged or spilled, BiteDash offers a choice of a full refund to the original payment mode or an instant 110% wallet credit.
- Missing Items: For missing items, a partial refund covering the exact cost of the missing item plus applicable tax will be refunded within 24 to 48 hours.

3. Wrong Order Received
- If a customer receives an incorrect item or another customer's order, BiteDash will immediately issue a full refund and arrange a priority re-order free of charge.

4. Food Quality & Health Concerns
- Taste preference or personal food likes/dislikes do not qualify for a refund.
- Reports regarding undercooked food, foreign objects, or food poisoning are immediately escalated to the Health & Safety Quality Manager.
