BITEDASH - OPERATIONAL & DELIVERY POLICY

1. Operating Hours & Service Coverage
- BiteDash operates 7 days a week from 8:00 AM to 11:30 PM.
- Breakfast menu is served from 8:00 AM to 11:00 AM.
- Night kitchen operations run until 2:00 AM in select areas (South & Central zones only).

2. Delivery Radius & Charges
- Standard delivery radius is 8 km from the partner restaurant.
- Free Delivery applies to all orders valued at ₹299 or higher.
- A flat delivery fee of ₹35 is charged for orders between ₹150 and ₹298.
- For orders below ₹150, a small order fee of ₹25 plus a delivery charge of ₹35 applies.
- Rain/Surge Fee: An additional ₹20 fee is added during heavy rainfall or peak demand hours.

3. Delivery Timelines
- Standard estimated delivery time (ETA) is 30 to 45 minutes.
- Express Delivery guarantees delivery within 25 minutes for select partner kitchens for a fee of ₹29.
- If an order is delayed by more than 25 minutes past the maximum estimated ETA (excluding rain delays), the customer receives a ₹50 BiteDash Wallet credit automatically.
