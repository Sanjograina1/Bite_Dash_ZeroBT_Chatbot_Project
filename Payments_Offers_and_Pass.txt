BITEDASH - PAYMENTS, FOODIE PASS & PROMOTIONS

1. Accepted Payment Modes
- Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, Amex).
- UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM).
- Net Banking and Mobile Wallets.
- Cash on Delivery (COD) is available only for orders up to a maximum limit of ₹1,200.

2. BiteDash "FoodiePass" Subscription
- Price: ₹99 per month.
- Benefits: 
  * Unlimited Free Delivery on all orders above ₹199.
  * Extra 10% discount up to ₹50 at top-rated partner kitchens.
  * Priority customer support escalation.
- Cancellation: FoodiePass can be canceled anytime from the profile settings. Auto-renewal can be turned off with zero penalty.

3. Coupon Codes & Referral Program
- Welcome Coupon: Use code 'FIRSTBITE' to get 30% off up to ₹75 on your first order (Minimum order value ₹199).
- Referral Bonus: Refer a friend using your unique code. Once they place their first order, both you and your friend receive a ₹100 wallet voucher valid for 30 days.
