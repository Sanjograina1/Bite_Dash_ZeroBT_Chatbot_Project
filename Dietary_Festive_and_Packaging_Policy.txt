BITEDASH - DIETARY GUARANTEES, FESTIVE MENUS & PACKAGING

1. "Green Dot" Pure-Veg Guarantee
- Dedicated Veg Kitchens: Partner restaurants with the "Green Dot" badge operate 100% vegetarian kitchens with zero meat presence on the premises.
- Green Delivery Bag Fleet: All pure-veg orders are delivered in distinct green insulated bags by dedicated delivery riders to prevent cross-contamination.

2. Navratri, Sawan & Jain Special Meals
- Festive Fasting (Vrat) Food: Prepared strictly using Sendha Namak (rock salt), buckwheat flour (Kuttu), and zero onion or garlic.
- Jain Food Option: Items marked with a "Jain" tag contain no root vegetables (potatoes, onions, garlic, ginger).

3. Packaging & Tamper-Proof Safety Seals
- Safety Seal Tape: All food packages are sealed with branded tamper-evident tape.
- Broken Seal Alert: If the safety seal tape is torn, cut, or tampered with upon arrival, customers MUST refuse to accept the delivery from the rider and report it immediately on the app for a 100% instant refund.
