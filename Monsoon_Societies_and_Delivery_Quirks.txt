BITEDASH - MONSOON, GATED SOCIETIES & RIDER SAFETY

1. Gated Society & Security Guard Delivery
- Society Entry Restrictions: If a gated society or apartment complex does not allow external delivery riders beyond the main gate, the delivery rider will wait at the security gate for a maximum of 10 minutes.
- Guard Handover: Customers can authorize the rider via in-app toggle to leave the food package with the security guard. BiteDash is not responsible for missing food once handed over to security.

2. Delivery OTP Verification
- OTP Requirement: Orders valued above ₹500 or placed between 11:00 PM and 5:00 AM require a 4-digit OTP shared by the customer to the delivery rider before handing over the food.

3. Monsoon, Heavy Rain & Waterlogging Policy
- Rain Surge Fee: A temporary surcharge of ₹25 to ₹50 is added during active rainfall warnings to compensate delivery partners.
- Extended ETA: Delivery timelines automatically extend by 30 to 45 minutes during waterlogging or heavy downpours. Delay-based compensation does NOT apply during official rain warnings.
